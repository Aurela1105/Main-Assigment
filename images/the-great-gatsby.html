About the book

The Great Gatsby is a story of ambition, romance, and the American dream. Set in the 1920s, it follows Jay Gatsby as he pursues his lost love, Daisy Buchanan.

Why I chose it

I love the vivid setting, the emotional longing, and the way the novel reveals the gap between appearance and reality.
