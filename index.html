Welcome

About this hobby

Reading has always been one of my favorite ways to explore new worlds, meet unforgettable characters, and learn something new without leaving my chair. From classic literature to modern fantasy, each book on my shelf tells a different story.

On this site you will find a curated list of my favourites books that left a lasting impression on me. Each entry includes a short description, a cover image, and a few extra details you can expand.

Top picks

These three titles are the books I recommend most often because they are rich, memorable, and easy to return to.

  • The Hobbit a warm fantasy adventure full of friendship and discovery.
  • To Kill a Mockingbird a moving story about kindness and courage.
  • 1984 a powerful, unforgettable tale about freedom and truth.

Genres

This collection includes familiar favorites from classic fiction, fantasy, dystopia, and gentle fables.

  • Classic fiction timeless stories with strong characters.
  • Fantasy imaginative worlds that feel both strange and welcoming.
  • Dystopian thought-provoking books that ask what society could become.

Reading tips

  1. Choose a quiet corner and read for just 10 minutes to start.
  2. Pause when a sentence feels strong and think about why it matters.
  3. Write one short note after each chapter to remember what you loved.
